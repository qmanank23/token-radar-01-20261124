Method — radar-2, in full

What this is not. Implied value is this entry's claim, not a price. “Records no token” is what DefiLlama's record says — it is not proof a protocol has no token. The model rates launch attractiveness, not protocol quality, and the snapshot cannot tell whether a token is actually planned. No farm step claims a points programme exists; step 5 tells you to check the protocol's own page.

Steelman the starter. radar-1 is 40 watchlist + 160 early = 200 rows with one formula, TVL × 0.25 with small growth and age terms. It is simpler, and if you only care about the top 5 by TVL it is nearly the same answer. radar-2 differs exactly here: the multiple is per-category and fitted from live-token comps, TVL is dampened by a fitted exponent, and growth and maturity are shown per row — so a young, growing protocol can outrank an old, shrinking one, and a category effect is flagged instead of hidden.
